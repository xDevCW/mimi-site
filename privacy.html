Privacy Policy

MIAR Studio Tools · Last updated: 21 October 2026 · Home

MIAR Studio Tools ("the app") is a private tool used by one person, the owner of a YouTube channel, to manage that channel. This policy explains what data the app accesses through Google and YouTube APIs, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Data the app accesses

When the owner signs in with their own Google account and grants permission, the app may access:

  • The owner's YouTube channel information (channel ID, title and the videos the owner has uploaded).
  • The owner's channel statistics and analytics (views, watch time, subscribers gained and similar figures).
  • The ability to upload videos, thumbnails, titles and descriptions to the owner's own channel.

The app does not access Gmail, Drive, contacts, calendar, or any other Google service. It does not collect data about viewers, commenters or any other third party.

2. How the data is used

Data is used only to provide the app's single purpose: to upload the owner's videos to the owner's channel, and to show the owner how those videos perform. It is not used for advertising, profiling, selling or any other purpose.

3. Sharing

The app does not sell, rent, transfer or disclose Google user data to any third party. Statistics may be sent as a private message only to the owner, through a messaging service the owner controls.

4. Storage and security

The OAuth access and refresh tokens that let the app act for the owner are stored only on infrastructure controlled by the owner, in access-restricted storage, and are never published or committed to public code. Channel statistics are kept only as long as needed to show the owner trends, and can be deleted by the owner at any time.

5. Retention, revocation and deletion

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation the app can no longer access the channel, and the owner deletes any stored tokens and statistics. For questions about data deletion, contact the address below.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app, the owner agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children and is not available to anyone other than its owner.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
